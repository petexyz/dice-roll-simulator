Normal quantile plot

Sort every roll from lowest to highest and split them into ten equal groups (deciles). Each dot is one decile: its normal score (z, where it would fall on a perfect bell) across the bottom, the total at that point up the side. If the rolls follow a normal distribution the dots lie along the straight reference line. With few dice the totals are coarse, so the dots form flat steps that stray from the line — a single die is a staircase, nothing like normal. As you add dice they settle onto the line.
