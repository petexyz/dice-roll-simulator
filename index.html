Dice Roller

Roll a handful of six-sided dice as many times as you like and see how the totals stack up.

Ready Choose your dice and press Roll.

Pick how many dice to throw and how many times to throw them. Each throw, the dice are added up to give a total. Press Roll to throw them. Press Clear to wipe the results.

Summary

A quick recap: how many times you rolled, the average total, the total that came up most, and the smallest and biggest totals you got.

Totals

Each bar is a total you could roll. A taller bar means that total came up more often. The short dark line on each bar shows how often that total should come up with fair dice. The orange curve is the bell curve (normal distribution) with the same average and spread as the dice. Point at a bar to see its numbers.
